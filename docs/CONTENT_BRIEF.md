## 6. Do not invent or publish without confirmation

- Years of experience or number of readings/clients.
- Formal education, certifications, affiliations, awards, or professional licences.
- Claims that tarot or numerology can cure illness, diagnose mental health issues, guarantee outcomes, or reliably predict an exact future.
- Specific numerology consultation plans or prices.
- Reiki, astrology, healing, crystal, Vastu, or other services not explicitly confirmed as currently offered.
- Prices, duration, online/offline format, opening hours, booking availability, payment options, cancellation policy, or refund policy.
- Testimonials, case studies, client identities, star ratings, review totals, or client logos.
- NGO name, title/role, cause, duration, impact figures, beneficiary stories, images, or endorsement.
- Precise location/house number, map pin, or office hours beyond the public listing currently visible.

## 7. Ethical wording and scope

Prefer:

- “Personal tarot reading”
- “Reflective guidance”
- “A space to explore a question”
- “A fresh perspective”
- “Your choices remain your own”
- “Enquire about a consultation”

Avoid:

- “Guaranteed future prediction”
- “100% accurate”
- “Fix your relationship/career/finances”
- “Cure depression/anxiety”
- “Guaranteed healing”
- “Remove all problems”

Suggested footer disclaimer:

> Tarot readings are offered for personal reflection and insight. They do not guarantee future outcomes and are not a substitute for qualified medical, mental-health, legal, or financial advice. Your decisions remain your own.

Have Archana review the disclaimer and have a qualified professional review legal/privacy language if the site starts collecting enquiries or payments.
